INDIVIDUAL-CUSTOMER MVP

End-to-end workflow

Summary of documented workflow. Timeouts, detailed conflict rules, verification responsibility and exception rules remain unresolved until stakeholders approve them.

  1. DiscoverCustomer finds a household or community service.
  2. RequestCustomer submits service, location, schedule and work details.
  3. ReviewCooperative accepts, rejects or requests clarification.
  4. RankSystem ranks suitable workers using transparent allocation rules.
  5. OfferManager sends an offer; worker accepts or rejects.
  6. AgreeRequired parties agree scope, schedule, price and payment terms.
  7. ConfirmConflict and authorization checks pass before confirmation.
  8. DeliverWorker updates progress; completion is recorded and verified.
  9. RecordPayment declarations, verification status and balances are maintained.

TEAM AND DELIVERY VIEW

Team and implementation roadmap

Team context

    These are documented skills, not assigned commitments.

    Proposed implementation sequence

    1. Resolve role, fairness, availability and state-transition decisions.
    2. Build the core request, worker, allocation and assignment workflow.
    3. Add negotiation, job completion and payment-status records.
    4. Harden authorization, audit, testing, responsive interface and backup/restore.
    5. Evaluate deferred features only after the pilot workflow is working.

    KEEPING SCOPE CLEAR

    Scope boundaries

    Included

    Individual customers; household/community requests; fair allocation; offers and responses; negotiated pricing where applicable; completion; payment-status recording.

    Later

    INS-01–INS-03: institutional accounts, requests and delivery tracking. Also Android, gateway processing and advanced analytics.

    Conditional

    INS-04: institutional contracts, tenders and procurement. Requires stakeholder validation before inclusion.

    Important business rules
    • An assignment offer is different from a confirmed assignment.
    • Availability does not automatically stop an offer; confirmation must satisfy approved conflict rules.
    • Fairness cannot bypass mandatory eligibility, suitability or safety requirements.
    • Payment records do not claim gateway processing or independently verified settlement without evidence.
